# Code of Conduct

Headscale Easy follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind, be patient, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the
work. Harassment, insults and discrimination of any kind are not tolerated in
issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer,
[Rafa Madolell](https://github.com/insanerask77), through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/insanerask77/headscale-easy/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message. Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may
remove content or ban participants who break these rules.
